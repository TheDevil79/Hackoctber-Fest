01 / About

A clearer view of your
external posture.

PatchLens is a defensive, non-invasive security posture scanner built to reveal the signals your public systems already expose.

It observes configuration and software fingerprints without exploitation, authentication attempts, or disruptive traffic. The result is a concise view of security hygiene that teams can use to investigate and harden their surface.
